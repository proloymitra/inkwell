How to Play

The desk. Seven letters are scrambled under the clue. They are not in word order. Tap them to build a word that fits the headline.

File word checks it. A hit fills the chip. A wrong word does not end the edition — it costs 10 seconds and clears the bar so you can try again.

The clock sits in the masthead. Easy is 1:30, Medium 2:00, Hard 2:30, Daily 3:00. Time left is added to the score when the word is filed. At 0:00 the press stops.

Skip sets this word aside and deals another. You may skip three times in a sitting. A skip does not add the time bonus.

Menu holds the clock. From there, Shuffle spins the letters and Hint spends a hint to reveal the start. Keyboard letters work too.

Daily Challenge is one shared puzzle for the calendar day — three minutes, no second edition. File it to keep your evening streak. Miss a day and the streak breaks. It holds until midnight once filed.

Byline is the name on your scorebook, kept on this device. A filed word is measured against your best on that desk.

Score = length × 120, plus time remaining, minus hints. A perfect desk earns a bonus column.
